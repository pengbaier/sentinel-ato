What we built

Sentinel: AI-powered account-takeover detection for risk teams

A B2B software service for financial firms and transaction platforms. It scores every login and session, explains each flagged case to an analyst, and decides: allow, MFA challenge, or lock.

In scope today

  • Account takeover at login and in-session
  • Case explanations for analysts
  • Manipulation-resistant support flows
  • Benchmark on public real-world login data

Out of scope today

  • Card-payment fraud and AML scoring
  • Insider and privileged-account monitoring
  • Production integrations and SLAs
